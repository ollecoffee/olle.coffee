The coffee was roasted by [Paper Cup Coffee](https://www.instagram.com/papercup.valencia/), an up-and-coming roaster based in Valencia, Spain.

Paper Cup is one of the newer ones, a roaster and a café in the same place. There is more specialty coffee in Valencia every year.

The coffee is a Lavado Guatemala, grown in the southern highlands of Guatemala. It is a washed 100% Arabica and the notes are milk chocolate, caramel and berries. It is a very clean and sweet coffee, and it works both as a V60 pour over and as an espresso.

They also make a horchata latte, espresso with horchata and cinnamon. That is the one I remember from the visit, more than the Guatemala.
