I found this bag at the Halles du Boulingrin in Reims on a whim, and it ended up being the best coffee I had all trip. Small towns keep doing that to me.

It's roasted by [Typikah](https://www.typikah.com/), an organic and specialty coffee roaster based in France.

Inside is a Timaná, grown in the Huila region of Colombia by Cata Reserva, the small operation run by Catalina and Pierre. Some coffees you just taste, this one you live. Catalina and Pierre took over the farm from Rodrigo Cortés and now look after 30 hectares split between protected forest and agroecology. It's a Castillo variety Arabica, grown at 2000 meters, hand picked, oxidized for 24 controlled hours, then slowly dried into a sincere, lively, and deeply human semi washed cup.

In the cup it's caramel and orange first, then chocolate and toasted nuts. Sweet, bright, and the kind of comforting you want to just sit with for a while.

Brewed a fresh pot, poured it into my favorite Moomin mug, and had myself a proper good morning.
