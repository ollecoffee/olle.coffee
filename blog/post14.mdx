The coffee was roasted by [Bluebell Coffee](https://bluebellcoffeeco.com), a roaster from Valencia, Spain.

The café is in the Russafa neighborhood. It is led by women, the baristas are women, and the beans are sourced from farms led by women.

The food is seasonal, avocado toast and pastries to go with the coffee.

I had a Mango Sweet Cream Cold Brew, mango and sweet cream over a dark cold brew.
