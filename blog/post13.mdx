The coffee was roasted by [Tallat Coffee](https://tallat.coffee), a roaster from Valencia, Spain.

A neighborhood café and roastery, right by the sea in El Cabanyal.

The notes are cherry, caramel and macadamia, with white chocolate underneath. It is a very sweet coffee, nutty and smooth.

A tallat is what the locals call a cortado.
